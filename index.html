About the Work

Independent, client-direct, and self-managed for two decades — from first sketch to final delivered file.

Illustrator and graphic designer with 20 years of independent freelance experience, working directly with clients — musicians, apparel brands, and individuals — to build visual identities, illustrations, and brand graphics from concept through final delivery.

Work spans album covers, tattoo design, apparel graphics, editorial layouts, and logos, with additional experience in web design. Comfortable managing every stage of a project solo: discovery, revisions, production, and client communication — backed by strong Adobe Creative Cloud skills and organized file delivery.

Self-directed projects include AV Magazine, a conceptual editorial publication exploring print and digital media, and Rage of Aquarius, a visual identity and merchandise campaign.
